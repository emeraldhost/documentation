To keep your game server running, renew its runtime in time. You can do this either manually with the **Verlängern** (Renew) button or automatically, so you no longer have to keep track of the expiry date.

## Check the Runtime

Open your server's dashboard. In the **Übersicht** (Overview), "Läuft ab in X Tagen" (Expires in X days) shows how long your server has left, e.g. "Läuft ab in 4 Tagen" (Expires in 4 days).

## Available Durations

From 90 days on, you get a discount that grows with the duration:

| Duration | Discount |
| -------- | -------- |
| 10 days | – |
| 30 days | – |
| 90 days | 5 % |
| 180 days | 10 % |
| 365 days | 15 % |
| 730 days | 20 % |

## Renew Your Server Manually

1. **Open the renewal**\
   Open your server's dashboard and click **Verlängern** (Renew) at the top. The **Server verlängern** (Renew server) dialog opens.

2. **Choose a duration**\
   In the **Laufzeit** (Duration) step, choose how long you want to renew your server. **30 Tage** (30 days) is preselected. At the bottom you see the **Gesamtbetrag** (Total amount) for the selected duration.

3. **Choose a payment method**\
   In the **Zahlungsmethode** (Payment method) step, choose how you want to pay.

4. **Check the overview**\
   Check your selection and the **Gesamtbetrag** (Total amount) in the **Übersicht** (Overview) and complete the renewal.

> [!NOTE]
> To switch to the Performance class, your server needs at least 14 days of remaining runtime. If the remaining runtime is shorter, renew your server first. Learn more in [Change Performance Class](/tutorials/gameserver/change-performance-class).

## Set Up Automatic Renewal

With automatic renewal, your server is renewed automatically shortly before it expires. For this you store a payment method, which is kept securely with our payment provider and charged shortly before expiry.

1. **Open automatic renewal**\
   Open your server's **Übersicht** (Overview). As long as your server is not renewed automatically, the **Automatische Verlängerung** (Automatic renewal) box shows the status **Inaktiv** (Inactive). Use this box to open the **Automatische Verlängerung** (Automatic renewal) dialog.

2. **Choose a payment method**\
   In the **Zahlungsmethode** (Payment method) step, choose which payment method should be used to renew your server automatically. You can choose between **PayPal**, **Kreditkarte** (Credit card), **Pay by Bank** and **EPS**.

3. **Start the setup**\
   Click **Weiter zur Einrichtung** (Continue to setup) and follow the steps up to the **Übersicht** (Overview).

> [!NOTE]
> Depending on the payment method, a verification amount of up to €1.00 is charged. We credit this amount to your balance.

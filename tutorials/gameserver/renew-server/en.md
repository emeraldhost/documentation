To keep your game server running, renew its runtime in time. You can do this either manually with the **Renew** button or automatically, so you no longer have to keep track of the expiry date.

## Check the Runtime

Open your server's dashboard. In the **Overview**, **Expires** in the **Server Information** box shows how long your server has left, e.g. "in 4 days".

## Available Durations

From 90 days on, you get a discount that grows with the duration:

| Duration | Discount |
| -------- | -------- |
| 10 days | – |
| 30 days | – |
| 90 days | 5 % |
| 180 days | 10 % |
| 365 days | 15 % |
| 730 days | 20 % |

## Renew Your Server Manually

1. **Open the renewal**\
   Open your server's dashboard and click **Renew** at the top. The **Renew Server** dialog opens.

2. **Choose a duration**\
   In the **Runtime** step, choose how long you want to renew your server. **30 days** is preselected. At the bottom you see the **Total** for the selected duration.

3. **Choose a payment method**\
   In the **Payment Method** step, choose how you want to pay.

4. **Check the summary**\
   Check your selection and the **Total** in the **Summary** step and complete the renewal.

> [!NOTE]
> To switch to the Performance class, your server needs at least 14 days of remaining runtime. If the remaining runtime is shorter, renew your server first. Learn more in [Change Performance Class](/tutorials/gameserver/change-performance-class).

## Set Up Automatic Renewal

With automatic renewal, your server is renewed automatically shortly before it expires. For this you store a payment method, which is kept securely with our payment provider and charged shortly before expiry.

1. **Open auto-renewal**\
   Open your server's **Overview**. As long as your server is not renewed automatically, the **Auto-renewal** box shows "Your server will not renew automatically" and the status **Inactive**. Use this box to open the **Auto-renewal** dialog.

2. **Choose a payment method**\
   In the **Payment method** step, pick the payment method we should use to renew your server. You can choose between **Credit card**, **PayPal**, **EPS** and **Pay by Bank**.

3. **Start the setup**\
   Click **Continue to setup** and follow the steps up to the **Review**.

> [!NOTE]
> Depending on the payment method, a verification amount of up to €1.00 is charged. We credit this amount to your balance.

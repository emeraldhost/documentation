The Owner ID is your Epic Games ID and is required for your server to start.

> [!TIP]
> Here you can find a guide on how to find your [Epic Games ID](/tutorials/gameserver/epicgamesid-find-out). You can also find your ID directly in-game under **Settings** at the bottom under **My Player ID**.

## Enter Owner ID

1. **Open dashboard**\
   Open the dashboard of your server.

2. **Open settings**\
   Navigate to the **Settings**.

3. **Enter Owner ID**\
   Enter your Player ID in the **Owner ID** field.

4. **Start the server**\
   Save the setting and start your server.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The server will **not start** until an Owner ID is set.

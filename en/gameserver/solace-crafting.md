# Solace Crafting

## What is Solace Crafting?

Solace Crafting is an open world survival RPG developed by Big Kitty Games. The game world is procedurally generated and practically endless: the further you travel from your starting point, the stronger the enemies become and the more valuable the resources you find. Progress is therefore not driven by a fixed story, but by the distance you dare to cover.

Gathering, crafting and base building are at the core of the game. You set up workshops and production buildings, improve your equipment piece by piece and turn your base into a safe foothold in the wilderness. On top of that come RPG elements such as character stats, skills and magic that you develop over the course of the game.

How your world is built is decided when you create it: difficulty, seed, biomes and various rates can all be configured up front. Every world is stored as its own folder, which is what makes it possible to move a world onto a server.

Solace Crafting can be played solo or together in co-op multiplayer. A dedicated server keeps your world available around the clock, so your friends can keep playing even when you are not online yourself. The server can either appear publicly in the server list or run privately and be joined via direct connection only.

The game is available on PC via Steam and mainly appeals to players looking for a calm, building-focused survival experience with a clear progression curve and a strong co-op element.

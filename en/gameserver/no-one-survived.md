# No One Survived

## What is No One Survived?

No One Survived is an open world survival game by developer Cat Play Studio. A virus has brought civilisation down: cities, villages and industrial sites lie abandoned, the streets belong to the infected, and you start without any equipment in a world where every night is more dangerous than the day.

The core of the game is scavenging, crafting and building your own base. You search houses, warehouses and vehicles for resources, craft tools, weapons and equipment, fortify a shelter and secure your supplies through farming, hunting and cooking. Hunger, thirst, exhaustion and a possible infection keep the pressure up at all times.

The infected react to noise and light, come in different variants and will also attack during the day if the server is configured that way. Almost every rule can be adjusted: the number and strength of the zombies, the length of day and night, loot amounts, the lethality of the virus, permanent death as well as PvP or pure PvE.

Several players can survive together on one server, share a base and split up tasks between them. No One Survived launched into Early Access in January 2023 and reached version 1.0 on 27 December 2025. The game is available on PC via Steam.

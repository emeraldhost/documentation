# Voyagers of Nera

## What is Voyagers of Nera?

Voyagers of Nera is a cooperative survival and adventure game set in a vast ocean world, developed by Treehouse Games. Players set sail together, explore scattered islands, gather resources and face the dangers above and below the surface. The game has been in Early Access on Steam since September 2025.

Sailing, exploration and building are at the core of the game. Players build and expand their boats, establish bases on islands, craft equipment and slowly work their way into unknown waters. Progress is stored persistently in the world, so you can continue your journey together at any time.

On your own server the world keeps running regardless of whether the original host is online. Up to ten players can share a single world. How many slots your server offers, which name it carries and whether it is protected by a password is set in the dashboard.

Voyagers of Nera is especially popular among players looking for a relaxed co-op experience with survival elements, sailing and shared exploration. The game is available on PC via Steam.

# Echoes of Elysium

## What is Echoes of Elysium?

Echoes of Elysium is a cooperative survival and crafting game developed by Loric Games and published by Snail Games USA. You explore a world above the clouds, gather resources, craft equipment and build your own airships to travel from region to region.

At the core of the game are building and piloting your own flying vessels. You construct your ship piece by piece from the materials you collect, equip it and upgrade it in order to reach more distant and more dangerous areas. On top of that come classic survival elements such as resource gathering, crafting and building up your own base.

Echoes of Elysium can be played solo or in online co-op with up to six players. On your own server your shared world stays available permanently, even when nobody is online — so you never have to wait for one particular player to launch the game.

The game entered Early Access on 27 January 2026 and is available on PC via Steam.

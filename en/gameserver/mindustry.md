# Mindustry

## What is Mindustry?

Mindustry is a top-down factory building and tower defense game developed by Anuken. You land on a map with a core, mine resources and connect drills, conveyors, factories and defensive structures into a working production chain.

Logistics are at the heart of the game: copper, lead, graphite and other materials have to be in the right place at the right time so your turrets keep firing, your factories keep producing and your core survives the incoming waves. Every wave sends stronger enemies, so you constantly need to expand and rebuild your base.

Besides wave-based maps there are attack and PvP maps where the goal is to destroy the enemy core. On top of that come units that you produce and can control yourself, as well as an extensive logic system for automated processes.

Mindustry is open source and can be played solo or together in co-op multiplayer. For permanent play there is a dedicated server that keeps your map available around the clock. Mods and plugins let you add content, maps and server commands.
